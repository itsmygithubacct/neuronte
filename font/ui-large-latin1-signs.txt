# x 0..10 is one em, y 0..20 down the cell; ascender 6.5, cap top 7.5,

# stroke; the ink of a character fits between 6 and 16. The pen is
# ui-large's (radius 3/64 em) except where a stroke names its own: the
# superscripts, the ordinals and the fractions are the face's digits and
# letters scaled by 0.6 or 0.5 and drawn at radius 0.036 em.
# The step to the next character is in font/ui-large-latin1-full-advances.txt.
# U+00AD, the soft hyphen, is drawn as the hyphen: a program that shows
# it at all shows a hyphen.
