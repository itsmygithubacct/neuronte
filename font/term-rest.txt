# in this order, are cut into NETS equal runs, one group net each. A

# moon from its phase). A "# note U+XXXX text" line says what is not sure
# about a drawing; the review sheet prints it beside the character.
#
# Right-to-left order is the terminal's work: every Hebrew letter is drawn
# upright in its own cell. The emoticons and pictographs are wide
# characters in Unicode; each is drawn to fill one cell.
