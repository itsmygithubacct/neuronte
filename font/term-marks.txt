# The characters of neuronte-term-marks-v1: the combining diacritical
# marks (U+0300 to U+036F), the Lao vowel signs and tone marks that sit
# over or under a letter, four modifier letters, the soft hyphen, the
# typographic spaces, the interlinear annotation characters, and 19 signs
# from Miscellaneous Symbols and Geometric Shapes drawn for one cell.
# Written for this project. No font was read: each drawing is made from
# the character's Unicode name and what its shape is known to be, with the
# project's own pen.
#
# The grid and syntax of strokes.txt (see train/strokes.py): x 0..10 across
# the cell, y 0..20 down it; cap top 4, x-height 8, baseline 15, descender
# 19. A terminal draws a combining mark in the cell of the letter before
# it, over that letter, so every mark here is drawn alone in the cell
# where it falls on a lowercase letter, as font/marks.txt draws them:
# marks above in y 3.3..5.6, marks below in y 16.6..19.3, both centred on
# x 5; an overlay crosses the middle of the x-height, y 11.5. A mark that
# joins two letters (U+035C to U+0362) is drawn from the middle of its cell
# to the right edge: the half over the first letter.
#
# Lao: the vowel signs above sit in y 4.4..6.9, over the letters' tops at
# 7.3; the tone marks and the cancellation mark go higher, y 1.6..4.2, so
# that a tone mark over a vowel sign stands clear of it, as Lao writes it.
#
# A "# set NAME NETS" line starts a training set: the characters after it,
# in this order, are cut into NETS equal runs, one group net each. A
# character line with no strokes is made by train/term_marks.py (a mark of
# font/marks.txt, a letter made small, a blank, a sign of
# neuronte-wide-symbols-v1 put in one cell). A "# note U+XXXX text" line
# says what is not sure about a drawing.
